Create a Grafana dashboard called "Talk Performance" using the Prometheus and Loki data sources. Set the default time
range to the last 1 hour with a 10s auto-refresh.

Include these panels:

Row 1 — Environment
* Panel 1 will be time series to plot both the CO2 and Temperature over time
    * Temperature comes from the metric: `co2meter_temperature_c`
    * CO2 comes from the metric: `co2meter_co2_ppm`
* Panel 2 will be a gauge of the current Temperature
* Panel 3 will be a gauge of the current CO2

Row 2 — Words Per Minute (full width time series)
* Panel 1 will be a time series of the words per minute with annotations for when filler words are used
    * Word count will come from the metric: `talkbox_words_total`
    * Annotations should occur from the Loki query: `{service_name="filler_words"}`
* Panel 2 will be a gauge of the average WPM within the time range
* Panel 3 will be the count of all filler words from the metric: talkbox_filler_words

Row 3 - Talk transcript
This is one large logs panel that will just contain the logs query: `{service_name="transcript"}`
